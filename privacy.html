Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: Pacey collects no data. Your drinks and nights stay on your iPhone. There's no account, no tracking, no ads and no Pacey server, so we never see any of it.

This policy covers the Pacey iPhone app (including its Lock Screen card and Apple Watch card) and this website. "We" means Pacey's developer, the seller shown on Pacey's App Store page. Questions? Contact us.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log lives in Pacey's storage on your iPhone:

  • the drinks you log and when, your nights and their summaries;
  • your settings, such as your limit, your drink list and your colour theme;
  • reports from past group nights.

We can't see any of this. Like every app's storage, it's included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or your computer) if you have those turned on. Those backups are between you and Apple.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code in the app.
  • No Pacey server. The app doesn't send your data to us or anyone else.
  • We never sell or share data, because we don't have any.

Group nights (only if you use them)

Group nights are optional. If you start or join one, Pacey uses your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) to share some details with the people in that group:

  • always: the name you enter, your seat emoji, your pace, the time of your last drink, your waters, cheers and water rounds, and whether you're home safe;
  • unless you turn them off: how many drinks you've had and what you're drinking.

Pacey never shares your standard drinks, your limit or your past nights. The group is stored in the iCloud account of the person who started it and shared with the people who join. We can't see it. A group night ends after 12 hours at most, and ending it deletes it.

Anyone with the group's invite link or QR code can join. So can anyone nearby with Pacey while someone in the group has the Group sheet open. Everyone who joins is listed by name.

Nearby: to find friends' group nights without a link, Pacey uses your local network (iOS asks first), which can reach phones close by over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. While the Group sheet is open it announces the group's name and invite link to phones close by. It doesn't connect to them or send anything else.

Alerts: group night updates and "home safe" alerts arrive through Apple's push notification service, set up through your iCloud. We don't receive your device's details.

Notifications

The morning-after "Your night summary is ready" notification is scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. Apple gives us standard sales reports (for example, how many tips were bought in which country), never your name, Apple ID or payment details. Tips unlock nothing. Refunds go through Apple.

The doctor report

The report is made on your iPhone. It only leaves your phone if you choose to share it (for example by email or AirDrop), and then it goes only where you send it.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They help us fix bugs and don't identify you. If you test Pacey through TestFlight, the feedback and screenshots you choose to send reach us through Apple.

Deleting your data

  • In Pacey: Settings → Delete all removes your nights and drinks.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone.
  • Group nights: leave or end the group. Ending deletes it for everyone, and groups end on their own after 12 hours.

Age

Pacey is for adults of legal drinking age where they live.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If Pacey ever starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and its date before that version comes out.
