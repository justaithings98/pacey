Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: the Pacey app collects no data about you. Your drinks and nights stay on your iPhone. There's no account, no tracking, no ads and no Pacey server, so we never see them. Group nights, if you use them, go through your own iCloud, not through us.

This policy covers the Pacey iPhone app (including its Lock Screen card, Dynamic Island and Apple Watch card), this website, and emails you send us. "We" and "us" mean Pacey's developer, the seller named on Pacey's App Store page. Questions? Contact us.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log is kept in Pacey's storage on your iPhone:

  • the drinks you log and when, your nights and their summaries;
  • your settings, such as your limit, your drink list and your colour theme;
  • reports from past group nights, which include the names and details the other people in that group shared.

We can't see any of this. Like any app's data, it's included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or your computer) if you have backups turned on, and Apple handles those backups under Apple's privacy policy.

Anyone who can use your iPhone can open Pacey and see your history. While a night is going, the Lock Screen card shows your count to anyone who sees your screen. You can hide the count or turn the card off in Pacey's Settings → Lock Screen card.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code in the app.
  • No Pacey server: the app never sends your drinks, nights or settings to us.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade personal information.

Group nights (only if you use them)

Group nights are optional and off until you start or join one. They use Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), through the iCloud accounts of the people in the group. While you're in a group, Pacey shares these details with everyone in it:

  • always: the name you type in, your seat emoji, your pace, the time of your last drink, how many waters you've had, cheers and water rounds you send, whether you're home safe, and when your details last updated;
  • on unless you turn them off when you start or join: how many drinks you've had and what you're drinking.

Pacey never shares your standard drinks, your limit or your past nights. Anyone with the invite link can also see the group's name and the starter's name.

Where it's kept: the group is stored in the iCloud account of the person who started it and shared with the people who join, like any shared iCloud item. Apple stores it, possibly outside your country, under Apple's privacy policy. We can't see it. As with any shared iCloud item, Apple may show the starter the iCloud name of each person who joins.

Who can join: anyone with the group's invite link or QR code, and anyone nearby with Pacey while someone in the group has the Group sheet open. Everyone in the group is listed by name, so check who's there. Only share the link with people you trust.

How long it's kept: when you leave a group, Pacey deletes your details from it. When the starter ends the group, Pacey deletes the whole group from their iCloud. Groups end after 12 hours: Pacey deletes the group the next time it runs on the starter's iPhone after that, and retries if a deletion fails. If the starter deletes Pacey before that happens, the group can stay in their iCloud until they remove Pacey's data in their iPhone's iCloud storage settings. Each person's iPhone keeps a short report of the group night (names and the details people shared) until they delete it or use Delete all.

Nearby: to find friends' group nights without a link, Pacey uses your local network (iOS asks your permission first). While the Group sheet is open in a group, your iPhone announces the group's name and invite link to devices close by on the same Wi-Fi or within Bluetooth range. Pacey doesn't connect to those devices or send them anything else, but someone nearby who receives the link could use it to join.

Alerts: group updates reach your iPhone through Apple's iCloud and push notification service. We never receive your device's details. Alerts such as "Ana is home safe" can show on your Lock Screen. You can turn them off in Pacey's Group sheet or in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications.

Notifications

The morning-after "Your night summary is ready" notification is scheduled on your iPhone and contains no counts. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. Apple gives us sales reports (for example, how many tips were bought in which country), never your name, Apple ID or payment details. Tips unlock nothing. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Things you choose to share

The doctor report and the group night story image are made on your iPhone. They only leave your phone if you share them (for example by email, Messages or AirDrop), and then they go only where you send them. Once shared, they're handled by that app and whoever receives them.

Crash reports and TestFlight

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us crash and performance reports. Apple doesn't include your name or Apple ID, and we use them only to fix bugs. If you test Pacey through TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you send, along with the name and email you joined TestFlight with.

If you email us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve Pacey, keep it no longer than we need to, never share it except as required by law, and delete it if you ask. Your email provider and ours (Google, for our Gmail address) also handle the message.

Your choices and rights

  • Pacey: Settings → Delete all removes your nights, drinks and group night reports. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.
  • Group nights: leave the group to delete your details from it, or end it if you started it.
  • Permissions (notifications, local network, Live Activities) can be changed any time in your iPhone's Settings → Pacey.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to complain to a privacy regulator (in Australia, the OAIC). We hold no app data about you, so for app data there's usually nothing for us to provide. For emails you've sent us, contact us and we'll help.

Age

Pacey is meant only for adults of legal drinking age where they live. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly receive information from children.

Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone's own security (your passcode and device encryption) and, for group nights, by Apple's iCloud. Keep your iPhone locked with a passcode. No system is perfectly secure.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. Links to other sites (Apple, GitHub, the OAIC) are covered by their own policies.

Changes

If we change how Pacey handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
