Common questions

Where are my drinks stored? Do you see them?

Only on your iPhone. Pacey has no account and no server, so we never see them. Group nights are the one exception, and only if you use them: they go through your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

Will my history move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you move to the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or with Quick Start. Pacey doesn't sync between devices on its own, and deleting the app deletes its history.

How do I log from the Lock Screen?

Once a night has started in the app, a Pacey card appears on your Lock Screen with a +1 button. If it doesn't, check that it's turned on in Pacey's Settings, and that Live Activities are on in your iPhone's Settings → Pacey.

How does a night start and end?

A night starts with your first +1. It ends when you tap End night, or by itself after a few hours with nothing logged. The next morning you get a short summary.

What's a "standard drink"?

In Australia, one standard drink is 10 g of alcohol. Pacey works it out from a typical size and strength for each drink. Real pours and brands differ, so treat it as an estimate. You can change any drink's size and strength in Settings → Drinks.

Why does Pacey ask me to tap "I understand" before each night?

It's a short reminder that Pacey's numbers are estimates, not medical advice, and must never be used to decide whether anyone can drive. It appears once before each night's first drink.

How do group nights work?
  • Everyone needs Pacey and to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone.
  • One person starts a group from the Group button on Home. Friends join by scanning the QR code, tapping the invite link, or picking it from the Nearby list while the Group sheet is open.
  • You choose whether to share your count and what you're drinking. Your standard drinks, limit and past nights are never shared.
  • A group ends after 12 hours at most. Ending it deletes it.
I can't join a group night.
  • Check you're signed in to iCloud (iPhone Settings → your name) and online.
  • For the Nearby list, allow Local Network for Pacey (iPhone Settings → Pacey), and ask a friend in the group to keep the Group sheet open.
  • An invite link stops working once the group has ended.
What do tips unlock?

Nothing. Pacey is free and everything works without paying. Tips just help keep it going. For a refund, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

In Pacey, go to Settings → Delete all. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on your iPhone.

Looking after yourself

Pacey isn't medical advice. If you're worried about your drinking, talk to your doctor. In Australia you can call the National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline on 1800 250 015, free and confidential, any time. In an emergency, call 000.
