Common questions

Where are my drinks stored? Do you see them?

Only on your iPhone. Pacey has no account and no server, so we never see them. Group nights are the one exception, and only if you use them: they go through iCloud. See the privacy policy.

Will my history move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or with Quick Start. Pacey doesn't sync between devices on its own. If you set up the new iPhone as new, or delete the app, the history isn't kept.

How do I log from the Lock Screen?

Once a night has started in the app, a Pacey card appears on your Lock Screen with a +1 button. If it doesn't, check that it's on in Pacey's Settings → Lock Screen card, and that Live Activities are allowed in your iPhone's Settings → Pacey. iOS can remove the card after several hours; open Pacey to bring it back.

Can people see my count on the Lock Screen?

Anyone who sees your screen can see the card. In Pacey's Settings → Lock Screen card you can hide the count (the +1 button still works) or turn the card off.

How does a night start and end?

A night starts with your first +1. It ends when you tap End night, or by itself after a few hours with nothing logged (6 by default; you can change this in Settings). The next morning you get a short summary.

What's a "standard drink"?

Pacey uses the Australian standard drink: 10 g of alcohol. Other countries measure differently (for example, a UK unit is 8 g and a US standard drink is about 14 g), so the number may not match your local guidelines. Pacey works it out from a typical size and strength for each drink, and real pours and brands differ, so treat it as an estimate. You can change any drink's size and strength in Settings → Drinks.

Why does Pacey ask me to tap "I understand" before each night?

It's a short reminder that Pacey's numbers are estimates, not medical advice, and must never be used to decide whether anyone can drive. It appears once before each night's first drink, and +1 waits until you've accepted it.

How do group nights work?
  • Everyone needs Pacey, an internet connection, and to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone.
  • One person starts a group from the Group button on Home. Friends join by scanning the QR code, tapping the invite link, or picking it from the Nearby list while the Group sheet is open.
  • You choose whether to share your count and what you're drinking. Your standard drinks, limit and past nights are never shared.
  • Anyone with the link can join, so share it only with people you trust, and check the names in the group.
  • A group ends after 12 hours at most, or sooner when the starter ends it.
I can't join a group night.
  • Check you're signed in to iCloud (iPhone Settings → your name) and online.
  • For the Nearby list, allow Local Network for Pacey (iPhone Settings → Pacey), and ask a friend in the group to keep the Group sheet open.
  • An invite link stops working once the group has ended.
What do tips unlock?

Nothing. Pacey is free and everything works without paying. Tips just help keep it going. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

In Pacey, go to Settings → Delete all. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored on your iPhone. To remove yourself from a group night, leave the group.

Looking after yourself

If you're worried about your drinking, or someone else's, talk to your doctor. In Australia, the National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline is free and confidential on 1800 250 015 (24 hours in most states and territories). In an emergency, call 000 in Australia, or your local emergency number.
